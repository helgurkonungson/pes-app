**Полное описание (≤4000, 1550):**
```
Chompbook turns anything you want to learn into short daily lessons — eat a whole book one bite at a time.

LEARN FROM YOUR OWN MATERIALS
• Add PDF, EPUB, Word and text files, web pages or your notes. Scanned pages are recognised right on your phone.
• Chompbook splits them into bite-sized parts and builds a lesson for every day: theory, questions and a short quiz.
• Pick how many minutes a day you want to study — 5, 15, 30 or more.

STUDY SMARTER
• A placement test skips what you already know.
• Spaced repetition brings your mistakes back at the right time.
• Every 5th lesson starts with a short check, and a final test closes the topic.
• Listen to lessons with your phone's voice.

AI IF YOU WANT IT
• Connect a free AI service in a few taps, or use your own key (Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter and more).
• AI writes clear explanations and quizzes from your sources and checks your open answers.
• No AI? Lessons from your own materials and the built-in courses work fully without it — even offline.

FIVE FREE COURSES INSIDE
How to learn effectively, AI literacy, data and spreadsheets, financial literacy and critical thinking — 8 lessons each, in 9 languages.

STAY MOTIVATED
Daily goal, streaks, XP, badges and gentle reminders.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your materials and progress stay on your phone; text goes only to the AI service you choose.

Available in English, Español, Deutsch, Français, 中文, हिन्दी, Русский, Українська and Беларуская. Light and dark theme, phones and tablets.
```
