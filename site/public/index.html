Apply what you learned

Why does rereading feel effective but help less than trying to recall?

When we reread, the text feels familiar, so it seems we already know it. Recalling makes the brain pull the information out on its own, and that is what strengthens memory.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is Chompbook free?

Yes. Lessons from your materials, the five courses, streaks and badges are free. Chompbook Pro is an optional subscription for AI lessons without your own key.

How does AI check my answers?

At the end of each lesson you answer an open question in your own words. AI compares it with the lesson's source and key ideas, gives a score out of 10 and explains what worked and what to improve. Without AI you can compare your answer with the model answer yourself.

Do I need AI or the internet?

No. Lessons from your own materials and the built-in courses work fully without AI, even offline. AI makes explanations and quizzes richer and checks open answers.

Which files can I add?

PDF, EPUB, Word, TXT, Markdown, HTML, web links and pasted text. Files from Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive can be picked in the phone's file chooser.

Where is my data?

On your phone. We have no accounts and no copy of your materials. Details are in the privacy policy.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones and tablets now; an iPhone version is planned. Light and dark themes.
