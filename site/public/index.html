Chompbook

Learn anything in daily portions. In Russian: «Грызи».

Chompbook turns your own books, articles and notes into a daily learning routine sized to the time you have — 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Each lesson reviews, teaches, quizzes and checks your thinking. Lessons work offline from your sources; AI is optional and uses your own key.
