How to turn a PDF or book into lessons and quizzes

Updated 10 November 2026

A textbook, work documentation or a book in PDF sits untouched for months, because reading 300 pages is hard to start. It's much easier when the material is split into short lessons with questions. Here are three ways to do it.

Why turn text into lessons

A short lesson with questions at the end makes you recall, not just read — the most effective way to remember (more in how to remember what you read). Small portions also remove the main obstacle: "no time to sit down with the book".

Option 1. By hand

  1. Split the text into parts of 5–10 pages.
  2. After each part, write 3–5 questions: "what is…", "why…", "how is it different from…".
  3. A day later, answer from memory; again a week later.

It works well but takes a lot of time: writing questions takes almost as long as reading.

Option 2. With an AI chatbot

You can upload the file to a chatbot and ask for questions on a chapter. The downsides: you have to write a prompt every time and remember where you stopped, there's no plan and no review, and the model's answers need checking — it can be confidently wrong. Writing prompts and checking AI answers is covered in the free course "AI Literacy".

Option 3. Automatically in Chompbook

  1. Add your material. PDF, EPUB, Word, TXT, Markdown, HTML, a web page link, pasted text, or photos and scans — text in scans is recognised right on the phone. Files from Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive are picked in the standard file picker.
  2. Choose your time: 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes a day. Lessons are sized to it.
  3. Take the placement test — it skips what you already know.
  4. Study in order. Each lesson has theory, a quiz and an open question checked by AI. Mistakes come back later.

What about privacy

Files and progress stay on your phone; there are no accounts. Text leaves the phone only for the AI service you chose: a free AI connected in a couple of taps, your own key, or Chompbook Pro. Details in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Can I make a quiz from a PDF for free?

Yes. Chompbook is free: for AI lessons you can connect a free AI in a couple of taps or use your own key. Chompbook Pro is only needed if you want AI lessons without your own key.

Does it work with scans and photos of pages?

Yes, text is recognised right on the phone, without sending images to a server.

How much text can I add?

A whole book: lessons go through it in order, part by part, until it's finished.
