Chompbook (in Russian: «Грызи») is a learning app made by Oleg Tsarkov, an individual entrepreneur registered in the Republic of Armenia (state registration no. 286.1553696, taxpayer no. 20064196, address: 12 V. Vagharshyan St., Arabkir, Yerevan 0012, Republic of Armenia) ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described below.

In short: your learning data stays on your phone. We run no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Text leaves the phone only to go to the AI service you choose (including Chompbook AI if you subscribe to Chompbook Pro), to us when you report a lesson or send an error report, or to a web page you ask the app to read.

This policy covers the app installed from Google Play, the Apple App Store or GitHub, and this website. If you installed the app from RuStore, the RuStore documents apply instead.

Data kept only on your device

  • Your skillsets, the books, articles, notes and courses you import, lessons, answers, progress, XP, streaks and badges.
  • Your name (as you typed it), daily goal, interface language, theme and reminder time.
  • An age group (under 13, 13–17 or 18+) if you connect an AI service or open Chompbook Pro. The birth year itself is not stored.
  • AI service keys, kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • An error log and your lesson reports, until you send or clear them.
  • Automatic backups in the app's storage and, before updates, in Documents/PES on the phone.
  • The phone's time zone is read on the device to suggest an AI service that works in your region; it is not sent anywhere.

We have no copy of this data and cannot see it.

AI services you connect

AI features are optional. If you connect an AI service (for example Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, Yandex Alice AI, Alibaba Qwen, or your own server), the app sends that service, directly from your phone and with your own key: excerpts of your materials, the skillset name and goal, your quiz results and the open answers you ask it to check. The service processes this under its own terms and privacy policy, which you accept when connecting it; for that processing the service, not us, is responsible. On some free tiers the service may use this content to improve its models — the app tells you so before you connect. Do not upload personal or confidential documents.

Google sign-in (only to create a free Gemini key for you): with your consent the app signs in to your Google account once, creates a "Chompbook" project with a key restricted to the Gemini API, and signs out. The access token, your e-mail address and your name are not stored.

OpenRouter sign-in (only to get a free OpenRouter key for you): you sign in on openrouter.ai; OpenRouter then sends a one-time code to chompbook.online/oauth/openrouter/, a page that only passes it on to the app on your phone and keeps nothing. The app exchanges the code for your key directly with OpenRouter. We never see your OpenRouter account or key.

Chompbook Pro and Chompbook AI

Chompbook Pro is an optional subscription sold inside the app from Google Play and, once released, the App Store. It adds Chompbook AI, an AI service run by us, so you don't need your own key.

  • Payment. You pay through Google Play or Apple under their terms and privacy policies. We never see your card details, name, e-mail address, Google account or Apple ID. The store gives us the order or transaction number, the plan, the country and currency, the price and the subscription status.
  • Anonymous ID. The app creates a random ID (such as $RCAnonymousID:…) that is not linked to your name, e-mail or store account. It identifies your subscription and your daily allowance.
  • RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) to confirm purchases with Google Play and the App Store. The app sends RevenueCat the anonymous ID, your purchase records for this app, the app and operating-system version, your locale and currency, and, like any internet request, your IP address. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf (its privacy policy). The app does not send it your advertising ID and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Lessons by Chompbook AI. When Chompbook AI writes a lesson or checks an answer, the app sends our server api.chompbook.online the same lesson content as for any other AI service (see the previous section) with your anonymous ID and whether you said you are 18 or older; for younger learners the server adds safety rules for the AI. The server checks the subscription with RevenueCat and passes the request to OpenRouter, Inc. (USA), which sends it to the company that runs the AI model (currently Anthropic; if it is unavailable, another model host). The answer comes back the same way. Our server does not store or read the content of your lessons, and we don't use it to train AI. OpenRouter is set to use only model providers that do not train on requests.
  • What our server keeps: the anonymous ID, whether the subscription is active and until when (a cached answer from RevenueCat), and how many AI tokens you used each day, so the daily fair-use limit can work. We keep no request logs. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes IP addresses to deliver and protect the service.

Lesson reports and error reports

Lesson reports. When you tap the flag to report a lesson, the report contains: the reason you picked, your optional comment, the text that was on the screen, the lesson title, which AI service and model wrote it, and the app version. It is saved on your phone and sent to us when you file it (if there is no connection, the app tries again later); in builds without our report server your mail app opens with a ready e-mail to support@chompbook.online instead.

Error reports. The error log (Settings → Error log) is sent to us when you tap "Send to the developer". Automatic sending is off by default; if you turn on "Send error reports automatically", new log entries are sent by themselves, at most once every 10 minutes. An error report contains the log entries (error messages and technical details), the app version, the platform, the interface language and your browser/WebView description, which includes the phone model.

In both cases anything that looks like an API key is removed, on the phone and again on our server. Reports go over HTTPS to chompbook.online/api/report, a server run for us by Cloudflare, Inc.; we store them in a Cloudflare database together with the country the request came from. Your IP address is used only for a moment to limit how many requests one device can send and is not stored. We receive a short notice about each report through Telegram. We use reports only to review content and fix the app. Please don't write personal details in the comment.

Other network requests

  • Web pages you add as sources are downloaded from their sites.
  • Update check (only in the version installed from GitHub): once a day the app asks GitHub for the latest release. Versions from the stores are updated by the store.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It remembers your language choice only in your browser. Cloudflare serves it and processes IP addresses to deliver and protect it.
  • Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address to the server you contact.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Error reports reach us only as described above.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information (including in the sense of California law), and we don't use your materials or lessons to train AI.

Why we process data and where

We process data to provide what you asked for (lessons, AI features, the Pro subscription), to keep the service working and fair (the daily limit, preventing abuse), and to review lesson and error reports you choose to send. Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR or a similar law applies, the legal bases are the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interests in a secure and working service (Art. 6(1)(f)). RevenueCat, OpenRouter, Cloudflare and AI model providers may process data in the USA and other countries; where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in their data-processing terms.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without AI (Settings → Lesson mode → "Always without AI").
  • Remove an AI key at any time in Settings.
  • Cancel Chompbook Pro at any time in the store: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or on iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Delete everything on the phone: Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. See Delete your data.
  • Export your data: Settings → Export backup.
  • Turn automatic error reports on or off at any time: Settings → Error log.
  • You may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of the data we hold (a lesson or error report you sent, or Pro data on our server), object to processing, or ask for a copy. For Pro data, include your order number (Google Play: starts with GPA.; Apple: from your receipt) so we can find the anonymous ID. We answer within one month. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Users under 13 cannot connect AI services or buy Chompbook Pro; lessons from their own materials work without sending anything. Users aged 13–17 may connect only services that allow their age, with a parent's or guardian's permission where the service requires it; purchases follow the family settings of their Google or Apple account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe we have, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS (except a server address you enter yourself starting with http://, such as a computer on your home network). Keys are kept in the phone's secure storage. Our server's own keys are stored as encrypted secrets at Cloudflare and are never in the app. Access to the report database is limited to the developer.

Changes

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date above. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app before they apply.
